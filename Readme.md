Marketing Performance Reporting & Acquisition Analysis

Overview

A marketing analytics reporting project built in Google Sheets to transform campaign-level performance data into a structured reporting system and interactive dashboard.

The project focuses on campaign and channel performance, acquisition KPIs, weekly reporting, and actionable marketing insights.

Tools Used
	•	Google Sheets
	•	Excel
	•	Pivot Tables
	•	KPI calculations
	•	Data cleaning
	•	Dashboarding
	•	Marketing performance analysis

Marketing KPIs

The analysis tracks:
	•	CTR — Click-through rate
	•	CPC — Cost per click
	•	CPM — Cost per thousand impressions
	•	Conversion Rate
	•	CPA — Cost per acquisition
	•	ROAS — Return on ad spend

Project Workflow

Raw Data → Data Cleaning → KPI Calculation → Campaign Reporting → Channel Reporting → Weekly Reporting → Dashboard → Findings

Dashboard

The dashboard includes:
	•	Total Spend
	•	Total Revenue
	•	Total Conversions
	•	Overall CTR
	•	Overall CPA
	•	Overall ROAS
	•	Spend vs Revenue by Channel
	•	Conversions by Campaign
	•	Weekly Revenue Trend

Key Findings
	•	Email generated the highest observed ROAS at 127.35X and generated approximately $42.89M in revenue.
	•	Paid Search generated 41.06X ROAS and approximately $12.41M in revenue.
	•	Affiliate generated approximately $5.48M in revenue with 16.81X ROAS.
	•	Display had the lowest observed ROAS at 0.35X and a CPA of $240.29.
	•	Paid Social and Video showed lower observed ROAS of 4.54X and 2.24X respectively.
	•	Overall, the dataset generated approximately $62.91M revenue from $1.88M spend, with an observed ROAS of 33.40X.

Analysis Limitation

The dataset does not include attribution windows, customer-level journeys, creative-level data, audience segments, or A/B test results. Therefore, channel performance should be interpreted as reported performance rather than causal incremental impact.

Deliverable

The Excel workbook contains the complete analysis, including raw data, cleaned data, KPI calculations, pivot reports, weekly reporting and the final dashboard.
